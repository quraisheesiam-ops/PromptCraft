THE FIRST STEP

So, what exactly is a prompt?

Before creating sophisticated prompts, let's understand the simple idea behind them. A prompt is simply how you communicate your intention to an AI system.

01 / UNDERSTAND
💭

An idea is not yet a direction.

Saying what you want is only the beginning. A strong prompt starts giving the AI useful information about what you want to see, how it should appear, and what kind of feeling the result should communicate.

SIMPLE IDEA

A beautiful forest.

02 / DIRECT
🎯

Give your idea direction.

Now we can introduce useful information: environment, perspective, lighting, atmosphere, composition, and visual character.

MORE DIRECTED

A dense forest during golden hour, viewed from a slightly elevated perspective, with soft sunlight filtering through layered foliage, natural depth between the trees, subtle atmospheric mist, and a calm, immersive cinematic feeling.

See what changed? 👀

The subject stayed the same. What changed was the amount of direction we gave the AI.

THE IDEA: A forest.

THE DIRECTION: A dense natural forest during warm golden hour, photographed from a slightly elevated viewpoint, with layered trees creating strong depth and natural leading lines. Soft sunlight filters through the canopy in delicate volumetric rays, illuminating textured leaves, moss-covered ground, and subtle atmospheric mist. The composition feels immersive and balanced, with a calm yet visually rich environment. Use natural earthy tones, realistic textures, cinematic depth, gentle contrast, and authentic environmental detail. The overall image should feel peaceful, believable, spacious, and visually captivating, while maintaining the untouched character of a real forest.
